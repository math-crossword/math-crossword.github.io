What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?
User Provided Information

The Application acquires the information you supply when you download and register the Application. Registration with the Service Provider is not mandatory. However, bear in mind that you might not be able to utilize some of the features offered by the Application unless you register with them.

The Service Provider may also use the information you provided them to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.


Automatically Collected Information

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile devices unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browsers you use, and information about the way you use the Application.


Where you came from, and your device model

When the Application starts, it records how it was installed and which device model it runs on, so the Service Provider can tell which of its marketing channels bring players. On Android this is the install referrer that Google Play provides, for example the campaign name when you installed after tapping an advertisement. On iOS this is the Apple Search Ads attribution, which tells only whether the installation came from an Apple Search Ads campaign and which campaign. Neither uses your advertising identifier, and iOS does not ask for tracking permission for it. The device model, for example the phone name, is stored with it. This information is linked to the random player identifier created by the Application and is not shared with third parties.


Gameplay activity

The Application records what happens in the game, for example which screens and boards you open, mistakes, hints, advertisements shown and purchases made, linked to the same random player identifier, so that the Service Provider can find out where players get stuck and fix it. The detailed activity log is deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask the Service Provider to delete the data linked to your player identifier at any time by contacting hungf.hp@gmail.com.


Game Progress Backup

So that a reinstall does not cost you your progress, the Application backs up your game progress (for example completed boards, hints and settings) to the Service Provider's server. The backup is keyed to a random secret that the Application creates and keeps in the device's secure storage: the iOS Keychain, or Google Block Store on Android. When you reinstall the Application on the same account or device, it finds that secret and restores your progress automatically. You are never asked to sign in. A backup that is not touched for 540 days is deleted automatically.


How to request deletion of your data

This section applies to the app Math Crossword Puzzle (also called Math Cross), developed by Hung Do. You can ask the Service Provider to delete the data it holds about you at any time, whether or not you still have the Application installed.

To request deletion:

  1. Send an email to hungf.hp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data - Math Crossword Puzzle".
  2. So that the Service Provider can find your data, include your player ID if the Application shows it to you. If it does not, include the device model, the operating system (Android or iOS), the approximate date you installed the Application, and the display name you used on the leaderboard, if any. The Application does not use accounts, so this is the only way to match a request to the right data.
  3. The Service Provider will confirm by email when the data has been deleted, within 30 days of receiving your request.

The following data is deleted when you make a request:

  • your player profile, including your display name;
  • your leaderboard entries;
  • your gameplay activity records;
  • your game progress backup;
  • the install source and device model recorded for you.

The following data is kept:

  • purchase records, which are kept in pseudonymized form (without your name or other directly identifying details) for as long as accounting, tax and refund obligations require;
  • aggregated statistics that no longer identify you, which may be kept indefinitely.

Some data is also deleted automatically without any request: the detailed gameplay activity log after 90 days, and a game progress backup that has not been used for 540 days. Uninstalling the Application stops all further collection, but does not by itself delete data already stored on the Service Provider's server, so please send a request as described above if you want it removed.
